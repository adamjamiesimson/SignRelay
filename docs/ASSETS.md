- **Teaching videos.** PopSign ASL v2.1, Thad Starner and Georgia Tech Contextual Computing Group with the Deaf Professional Arts Network, [dataset and licence](https://signdata.cc.gatech.edu/view/datasets/popsign_v2_1/index.html), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The asset pins and copied preprocessing preserve the upstream model contract.
