# SignRelay alpha: testing guide for signers

Thank you for helping. This guide is for ASL signers taking part in a **supervised** test session with a facilitator. The facilitator can share it in advance, or read or sign it with you on the day.

## What SignRelay is right now

- **An early research tool, not an interpreter.** It recognises a small number of ASL signs from a camera, and it often makes mistakes.
- **Words you signed with your own taught signs** ("personal signs") appear in the transcript straight away.
- **Everything else appears as a card first.** That includes the built-in sign rules (HELLO, THANK YOU, PLEASE, SORRY, YES, NO) and the research models. A card says *Possible sign* or *Experimental suggestion* and **is not in the transcript** until you press **Add**. If you do nothing, it disappears after about 12 seconds.
- **Nothing is spoken** unless you added the word and "Speak each word" is on.
- **The camera stays on your device.** The test session records no video, pictures, hand landmarks or your name. It keeps only what SignRelay showed, what you added or dismissed, and what you tell us went wrong. That record is saved only if you agree.

## Before you start

1. Sit so your face, upper body and both hands are visible, about an arm's length or two from the camera.
2. The facilitator will go through the consent choices with you. Each one is separate, and you can say no to any optional item. You can stop at any time.
3. The facilitator starts the session and the camera. Choose **American Sign Language** if asked.

## The tasks (about 25 minutes)

For each task the facilitator shows an instruction and presses **Start task**. You sign, decide about any cards as you normally would, and the facilitator presses **Done**.

| Block | What you do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Starter signs | Sign HELLO, THANK YOU, PLEASE, SORRY, YES and NO, three times each: at your natural pace, a little slower, a little faster. | Does SignRelay offer the right word? |
| 2. Everyday movements | Adjust your glasses, push hair aside, wave to someone, scratch your chin, touch your chest, drink, type or use your phone, keep your hands still. **Do not sign.** | Does SignRelay wrongly offer a word when you are not signing? |
| 3. Other signs | Sign HAPPY, BAD, SAD, PLAY, PARTY, COMPUTER, FOOTBALL, FUTURE, FEW and PROBLEM. | These have fooled the built-in rules before. Does it offer a wrong word? |
| 4. Unsupported signs | Sign two signs the facilitator chooses. | SignRelay should stay silent for signs it does not know. |
| 5. Short sequences | Sign, for example, HELLO, PLEASE, THANK YOU one after another, without pausing. | Can you keep up with the cards when signing quickly, and are added words in the right order? |

**Please sign naturally.** Don't slow down or exaggerate for the camera unless the task asks you to. We want to see what happens with real signing.

## Deciding about a card

- **Add only words you actually signed.** If a card shows a word you did not sign, press **Dismiss** or ignore it. Adding wrong words to see what happens is fine if you tell the facilitator, but please don't do it by habit; we are measuring how often that happens.
- **Add can't be pressed straight away.** The button only works about half a second after a card appears, or after it moves, so a tap meant for another card cannot add it.
- **Cards wait while you look at them.** While your pointer or keyboard focus is on the cards, they do not time out. New words then wait their turn ("1 more waiting").
- **Words are never lost silently.** Up to three cards are shown, in the order you signed. Words that time out or are pushed out are listed under **Not added: …**.
- **Added words keep their signing order.** An added word goes where you signed it in the transcript and is labelled **added by you**. You can remove or edit any word.
- **Keyboard:** Tab to a card's **Add** or **Dismiss**, then press Enter or Space. Screen readers announce each new card as "not added".

## Reporting what went wrong

After each task, choose what happened:
- **SignRelay showed what I signed**
- **It showed a different word.** Please also choose what you signed from the list.
- **It showed nothing for my sign**
- **It showed a word when I was not signing it**
- **Camera or hand-tracking problem**
- **Nothing was expected and nothing was shown**

Anything else, such as confusing wording, cards that were hard to reach, or something that felt wrong, please tell the facilitator. They will note it without your name, and only if you agreed to anonymous comments.

## After the session

- If you agreed to share the session record, the facilitator downloads it and keeps it privately. Otherwise it is deleted when the session ends.
- **To withdraw later**, contact the facilitator with the date of your session. Your records are deleted within 30 days and left out of all future results.
- Results are reported only as totals across several signers, never per person.
