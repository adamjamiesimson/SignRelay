# Research and data

- **PopSign ASL v1.0** published a 250-class isolated sign benchmark on portrait Pixel 4a recordings by 47 Deaf ASL signers. The paper held out 8 signers each for validation and test and used the remaining 31 for training. Its two-layer bidirectional LSTM reached 82.1% validation and 84.2% test top-1. It was trained at corpus scale and evaluated with isolated prompts. The official archives are CC BY 4.0. SignRelay uses publisher-reviewed game entries and preserves attribution and the held-out reservation. [Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/00dada608b8db212ea7d9d92b24c68de-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html) · [Supplement](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/00dada608b8db212ea7d9d92b24c68de-Supplemental-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf) · [Dataset](https://signdata.cc.gatech.edu/view/datasets/popsign_v1_0/)
- **PopSignAI** compared 1D-CNN, 2D-CNN and LSTM candidates for a constrained game on phones. The authors report 82.9% on their 250-sign LSTM test and 99.6% at about 7 ms for their constrained, five-class game model. They chose a 2D-CNN for the game. The local approximation uses the paper’s 60-frame padding and cropping, 21 two-dimensional landmarks, left-handed mirroring, and forced selection among five supported labels. It fits a smaller 1D CNN on the available subset with the Rebirth tracker. Full reproduction requires the complete benchmark splits, the original tracker and model configurations and a matching evaluation protocol. [PopSignAI paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3742413.3789164)
- **GestureSign** publishes a small Transformer, an ONNX Web app with MediaPipe Holistic and a signer-split training pipeline over the Kaggle PopSign landmark subset. Training reportedly used around 94,000 clips. Its browser feature parity fixture is relevant to V2. A full reproduction depends on the competition package and a suitable training runtime. These GestureSign results are maintainer-reported. [Implementation and model card](https://github.com/shashank11yadav/GestureSign) · [Competition data](https://www.kaggle.com/c/asl-signs/data)
- **PopSign v2.1** offers broader isolated-sign coverage and variants under CC BY 4.0. Its signer identities and archive members must be reconciled against v1.0 before treating it as independent. **ASL Citizen** offers 83,399 videos, 2,731 classes and 52 signers under noncommercial, non-revenue-generating research terms with redistribution restrictions. **WLASL** offers a valuable RGB and pose benchmark with separate data use terms. V2 weights contain no ASL Citizen, WLASL, MS-ASL or Sem-Lex data. [PopSign v2.1](https://signdata.cc.gatech.edu/view/datasets/popsign_v2_1/index.html) · [ASL Citizen data](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/asl-citizen/) · [ASL Citizen terms](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/asl-citizen/dataset-license/) · [WLASL](https://github.com/dxli94/WLASL)

## Implementation and evaluation protocol

All three model families share a 16-step, timestamp-resampled input contract, a learned temporal convolution, four temporal pooling regions and a linear output layer. Landmark input keeps both hand slots, presence masks, aspect-corrected hand and fingertip geometry, and shoulder and nose references from pose. RGB input uses per-frame RGB samples from a hand-centered crop. It uses tracking for crop location, leaving handshape classification to pixels. The hybrid network trains on concatenated landmark and RGB inputs. Each candidate uses the same training labels, recording membership, signer folds and calibration objective.

Training uses 122 clips from 31 signers. The development comparison uses 68 clips from 4 different signers, including 40 target signs and 28 unsupported signs. Intermediate failures count as abstentions where the original clip decoded and hand evidence fell below the tensor requirements. The negative class is composed of real unsupported signs. Ordinary movements, partial signs and consecutive signing require a reviewed collection. Publisher clip edges give the bounded comparison a fixed unit of evaluation. Reviewed sign onset and completion times are unavailable for these clips.

Four training signer folds produce out-of-fold scores for pooled raw-logit and margin cutoffs. The cutoff search targets no more than 12% acceptance of the training-fold unsupported clips and rewards correct accepts while penalizing wrong accepts. Reported development metrics include rejected clips in recall. Stream precision uses one trial-level error per incorrect event sequence, and the ledger records duplicate-output trials. The sampled corpus supports iterative development only.

Landmark and RGB records stay local in ignored directories. Redistributed artifacts consist of fitted parameters and aggregate evidence.
